Privacy notice

This is an English translation for convenience; the Spanish version prevails.

Toriku («the studio»), domiciled in Mexico City [legal name and address to be confirmed], is responsible for processing the personal data you provide to us, under Mexico’s Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties (LFPDPPP).

Data we collect

Name, email, company or agency and the project information you write in the quote form. We do not collect sensitive data. Payments are processed by Stripe; the studio neither receives nor stores card data.

Purposes

Primary: preparing your quote, booking the studio date, coordinating the production and issuing the invoice. Secondary (you may opt out): sending you studio news. If you do not want the secondary purposes, write to hola@toriku.wtf.

Transfers

We only share data with providers needed for the service (payment processor, invoicing and email), who process it on the studio’s behalf.

ARCO rights

You may access, rectify, cancel or object to the processing of your data, or withdraw your consent, by writing to hola@toriku.wtf with your name, the right you are exercising and a copy of your ID. We reply within 20 business days at most.

Cookies, measurement and advertising

Country and language cookie. When you arrive we ask whether you accept cookies. If you accept, we store our own cookie toriku_cookies (180 days) with your choice and your country code, which we identify without GPS, only from the network or your browser’s time zone, to show you a single flag and your language: if you are in Mexico or Spain you will see the site in Spanish with your flag. In any other case (another country, country not identified, or if you decline) the site is shown in English with the United States flag; if you decline, we only store that refusal. You can change your choice by tapping the flag in the header on the home page.

Some pages of this site show Google AdSense ads. Third parties, including Google, use cookies and device identifiers to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. Google’s advertising cookies let Google and its partners show you ads based on your browsing on this site and other sites on the internet.

We do not show ads on the quote form, account-based gear rental, the confirmation page or the error page.

You can opt out of personalized advertising at adssettings.google.com, and from other providers at youradchoices.com or youronlinechoices.eu. More on how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
