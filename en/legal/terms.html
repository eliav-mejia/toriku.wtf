Booking terms

This is an English translation for convenience; the Spanish version prevails.

1. Quote

No account is needed to get a quote. The quote form’s estimate is indicative and includes VAT (IVA). Toriku confirms the final quote by email; it is valid for 15 calendar days.

2. Deposit and date

The date is booked once the deposit is received: 30 % of the estimated total, VAT included (the quote form shows the amount). The balance is paid no later than the delivery day.

3. Hours and overtime

A shoot day is 10 hours, including setup and teardown. Each overtime hour is charged at 15 % of the day rate of the crew hired.

4. Changes and cancellations

You may move the date once at no cost with at least 72 hours’ notice, subject to availability. With less than 72 hours, or if you cancel, the deposit is non-refundable.

5. Gear rental

Renting gear without a service requires an account registered with an email. Prices are per item and per day; cameras are always rented with an operator from the network, charged separately. Gear rental does not include travel expenses (meals, lodging and transport outside the local area): they are calculated and charged separately. Availability, security deposit and delivery are confirmed by email. The client is liable for damage to or loss of rented gear.

5 bis. Optional studio

If the quote includes a studio with cyclorama, the client is liable for damage to the studio caused by their equipment or talent; repainting due to color or special materials is quoted separately.

6. Deliverables and rights

Delivery times and the scope of the image usage license are set in each project’s quote.
