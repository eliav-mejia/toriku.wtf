«Atrápelo quien pueda»: Jaime Camil's romantic comedy reaches cinemas

Released November 12, 2026: director, cast, story, production and why romantic comedy is still key to Mexican cinema's box office.

November 15, 2026 · 2 min read · Toriku team

On November 12, 2026, Atrápelo quien pueda (roughly, Catch Him If You Can) reached Mexican cinemas: a romantic comedy directed by Alfonso Pineda Ulloa, starring Jaime Camil and Natalia Téllez, with Tamara Vallarta in the cast.

The story

Diego is 45, a widower who has devoted his life to work and to raising his 11-year-old daughter Marina. The film follows his clumsy, endearing return to the game of love. It is a classic premise of the genre: a character who has closed himself off and a story that forces him to open up.

The production

The film is a co-production of Mediapro Studio US & Canada, Telemundo Studios and Cinépolis, and is distributed by Cinépolis Distribución. Shooting began in late September 2025, and the film had its first public screening at the Morelia International Film Festival in October 2026.

This production model is interesting in itself: an exhibition chain that co-produces and distributes, together with studios aimed at the US Hispanic audience. It is a route that seeks box office in Mexico and a later life on television and platforms in both countries.

Why romantic comedy matters

In a market where Mexican cinema takes a small fraction of the box office, comedy, and in particular romantic comedy with well-known faces, has historically been one of the genres that sells the most tickets for national productions. Its success or failure in cinemas is a sign of how audiences respond to Mexican films against the month's big franchises.

Under the new Federal Law of Film and Audiovisual Media, a Mexican film is guaranteed a minimum run of 14 days. The opening weekend is still decisive for how many screens and showtimes it keeps afterwards.

What the first reviews say

Some specialist outlets have highlighted the chemistry between Camil and Téllez. As always, we recommend reading several reviews and forming your own opinion in the cinema.

What to watch if you make films

  • Comedy timing: how long each scene runs and where the joke is cut.
  • Directing actors with children: the father-daughter relationship is the heart of this kind of story.
  • The cinematography of the city: how a «romantic» city is built for the screen.
  • The release strategy: festival, press, wide release and then platforms.
