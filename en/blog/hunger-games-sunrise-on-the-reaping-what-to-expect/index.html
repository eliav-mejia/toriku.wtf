«The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping»: what to expect from the release

The Haymitch prequel arrives in November 2026 with Francis Lawrence directing. Cast, story and what a filmmaker can learn from a prequel.

November 15, 2026 · 2 min read · Toriku team

The Panem saga returns to cinemas in November 2026 with The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping, the adaptation of Suzanne Collins's novel published in 2025. Sources place the Mexican release on November 19 or 20; confirm with your cinema.

The story

The film is set on reaping day for the 50th Hunger Games, about 24 years before Katniss's story. The lead is a young Haymitch Abernathy, the alcoholic mentor we met in the first films. Knowing what he will become is exactly what gives weight to every decision he makes.

Who is making it

  • Director: Francis Lawrence, who has directed the saga since Catching Fire.
  • Screenplay: Billy Ray.
  • Cast: Joseph Zada as Haymitch, Whitney Peak as Lenore, Elle Fanning, Kieran Culkin, Maya Hawke as Wiress, Glenn Close and Billy Porter, among others. International outlets have also reported that Jennifer Lawrence and Josh Hutcherson return in their original roles, in appearances being kept under wraps.

The challenge of a prequel

A prequel has a structural problem: the audience already knows how the character ends up. Good prequels turn that into an advantage:

  • Dramatic irony: every hope the character has hurts more because we know it will break.
  • The how, not the what: the question is not whether Haymitch survives, but what it costs him.
  • Visual echoes: shots, colors and songs that connect with what is to come.

They are useful tools for any screenwriter, even in a short.

What a short filmmaker can learn

  • Director continuity: having the same director across several installments gives visual coherence. In your own filmography, coherence also builds identity.
  • Production design as narrative: the contrast between the districts and the Capitol tells the story before the dialogue does.
  • Casting new faces alongside well-known names: a strategy that also works in independent film.

Box office and context

It is one of the most anticipated films of the season in Mexico. Its dates coincide with the launch of GTA VI, an unusual clash of releases between film and video games that says a lot about how both media now compete for the audience's time.
